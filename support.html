Quick answers

How do I restore Pro?

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the same store account used for the original lifetime purchase. If access is not restored, contact support with your app version and the store used. Never send passwords or full payment-card details.

Where is my shop data?

Jobs, notes, photos, tools and settings are kept locally on your device. Use Settings to export a full backup before uninstalling or moving devices. Exported files remain wherever you save or share them.

Does BlueChip work offline?

Core calculators, bundled references, notes and bundled macro content work offline. Purchases, restoring access, advertising and sending email require the relevant online services.

Can I download the public Android release?

Google Play production access is currently under review. Check the updates page for release status.
