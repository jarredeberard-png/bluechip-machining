UPDATED OCTOBER 18, 2026

Android public release: pending

The closed-testing period is complete and the application for Google Play production access has been submitted. Public availability depends on Google approval and final release checks. BlueChip is not yet publicly available on Google Play.

Current testing build: 1.2.6

The current Android testing build includes calculator input navigation, daily Pro information, revised launcher artwork, and refinements informed by machinist feedback. Test-build availability does not mean the public release is live.

Already in the app

  • Machining calculators with saved inputs, fraction support and Favorites
  • App-wide search, reference charts and CNC guides
  • Editable offline shop notes
  • Pro jobs, setup sheets, tool data, photos and PDF exports
  • Bundled offline CNC macro programs and Pro programming guides
  • Dark/light themes, local backups and in-app feedback

What comes next

Complete native advertising and final release verification, finish store declarations, and prepare the production build. Live advertising also depends on AdMob readiness review. Further improvements will follow real shop feedback; there are no announced dates for additional features.
