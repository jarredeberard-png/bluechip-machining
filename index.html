About BlueChip

BlueChip Machining is a machining reference app built to make life easier for CNC programmers, machinists, apprentices and manufacturing professionals.

Our goal is to provide practical calculators, programming references, and useful shop-floor tools in one easy-to-use application.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

BlueChip Machining does not require users to create an account.

Notes and settings created inside the app are stored locally on your device.

If you choose to send feedback, your device's email application will open with the message pre-filled. Any information you include is voluntarily provided by you.

BlueChip Machining does not sell your personal information.

We may update this Privacy Policy as the application evolves.
