BlueChip Machining Privacy Policy

BlueChip Machining ("BlueChip", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the BlueChip Machining mobile application handles and how it is used.

Information We Collect

BlueChip Machining does not require users to create an account and does not currently collect personal information through an account system.

Local App Data

Notes you create in the app and app preferences such as dark mode and your preferred unit system are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to BlueChip Machining.

Suggestions and Feedback

If you choose to send a suggestion or report an issue, BlueChip Machining may open your device's email application with an email address, subject and your message pre-filled. Sending that email is optional and requires you to complete the send action in your email application. Any information you include in the email is voluntarily provided by you and may be processed by your email provider.

Analytics and Advertising

BlueChip Machining does not currently include third-party advertising and does not currently use third-party analytics to track your activity inside the app.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information related to app downloads, installation, updates, security and store usage under Google's own terms and privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

BlueChip Machining is intended as a machining and manufacturing reference tool and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as BlueChip Machining changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact BerardIndustries@gmail.com.
